# PRL editorial memo

## Significance

At a continuum channel opening, suppression of normal flux is weaker than
suppression of the field; a strict embedded eigenstate exists only after the
nondecaying threshold amplitude and all finite-flux amplitudes vanish.

## Novelty

The manuscript formulates and realizes this amplitude-level condition in
scalar underwater acoustics through independent cancellation of one grazing
and one propagating diffraction channel.

## Priority-safe positioning

Do not claim the first BIC in water or the first Rayleigh BIC. Underwater
Fabry-Perot BICs and an electromagnetic Rayleigh-BIC construction already
exist. State instead that this work establishes a strict scalar-acoustic
Rayleigh-threshold BIC and exposes its channel-resolved double-cancellation
condition.

## Claim ranking

1. Square-integrability and amplitude cancellation at a diffraction threshold.
2. Strict acoustic realization at the Rayleigh branch point.
3. Two-source-family cancellation of threshold and finite-flux radiation.
4. Broader route to channel-opening acoustic embedded eigenstates, stated as
   a bounded framework rather than a universal theorem.
5. The underwater two-groove structure, used as the realization rather than
   the headline.

## Main-text allocation

- Keep: threshold norm argument, channel topology, strict amplitude zeros,
  full-operator null, pole continuation, strict field, exterior-norm
  saturation, two phasor closures, and one qualified single-groove comparison.
- Move to SI: Q exponent and detailed pole samples, convergence tables,
  fabrication tolerance, dimensional scaling, sound-speed sweeps, driven
  routing, anomalous reflection, reciprocity and unitarity audits, and the
  proposed experiment.

## Reviewer risks

- BIC specialist: may call the state a Rayleigh anomaly. Answer with the full
  homogeneous null, zero threshold amplitude, and saturated exterior norm.
- Acoustic specialist: may regard water as a platform translation. Explain
  that scalar pressure isolates the amplitude-versus-flux distinction without
  electromagnetic polarization or anapole physics.
- PRL editor: may find the paper report-like. Keep the main argument to four
  steps: threshold problem, strict state, independent proof, double-cancellation
  mechanism.

## Claim boundaries

The model is infinite, rigid, and lossless. The single-cavity result is a
bounded rectangular-family ablation. The local Q exponent is not universal.
The driven response does not establish a converged one-way device.
