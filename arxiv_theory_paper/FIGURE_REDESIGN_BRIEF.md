# PRL four-figure redesign brief

The current PDFs remain numerically valid. This brief defines the next visual
reassembly without requesting new calculations or changing any reported data.

## Figure 1. From a conventional BIC to a Rayleigh BIC

Figure-level claim: conventional BIC cancellation acts on a fixed set of open
channels, whereas a channel opening creates a zero-flux but nondecaying field;
a strict Rayleigh BIC must additionally cancel that Rayleigh-channel
amplitude.

- (a) Conventional BIC: symmetry or destructive interference removes the
  amplitudes of a fixed set of propagating channels.
- (b) Rayleigh anomaly: `k_y=0` and zero normal flux, but `A_RA != 0`; the
  grazing pressure remains nondecaying and the exterior norm grows with
  height.
- (c) Rayleigh BIC: two source families cancel both `A_RA` and the
  remaining finite-flux open-channel amplitude, yielding a localized
  homogeneous eigenstate.

## Figure 2. Acoustic realization

Figure-level claim: the two-groove cell realizes both radiation zeros at the
Rayleigh opening.

- (a) Two-groove cell. Describe the large groove as the dominant storage
  region and the small groove as an additional phase pathway. Avoid claiming
  a unique one-to-one modal role unless directly decomposed by the calculation.
- (b) Floquet channel topology and reciprocal exchange of the two first
  orders under angle reversal.
- (c) BIC point on the angle-frequency or `(kappa,Omega)` Rayleigh line.
- (d) Homogeneous `|A_-1|` and `|A_0|` reaching a common zero. Prefer these
  physical amplitudes to an optimizer-objective plot.

## Figure 3. Evidence for a genuine threshold BIC

Figure-level claim: independent spectral, operator, and spatial tests identify
a real-frequency localized eigenstate rather than a threshold anomaly or a
high-Q surrogate.

- (a) Complex pole approaching the Rayleigh branch point.
- (b) Full-operator null and homogeneous channel amplitudes.
- (c) Hero panel: strict homogeneous `|p(x,y)|` field.
- (d) Exterior norm for strict BIC, near-BIC surrogate, and ordinary
  resonance. Put detailed convergence and the local Q exponent in the SI.

## Figure 4. Double-cancellation mechanism and boundary

Figure-level claim: the two groove-supported source families close the
threshold and finite-flux radiation sums separately.

- (a) Complex-plane phasor closure for `A_-1^hom`.
- (b) Complex-plane phasor closure for `A_0^hom` using the same groove colors.
- (c) Single-rectangular-groove versus two-groove residual. Label this as a
  bounded ablation, not a universal no-go theorem.
- (d) Constraint-to-phase-pathway schematic. If space remains, add only a
  small dimensional-similarity inset.

## Move out of the main figures

- modal truncation tables and raw residual histories;
- fabrication Monte Carlo distributions;
- sound-speed and scale sweeps;
- driven routing and the off-Rayleigh anomalous reflector;
- proposed water-tank protocol.

These belong in Supplemental figures because they validate, delimit, or
motivate the result without establishing the central state.
