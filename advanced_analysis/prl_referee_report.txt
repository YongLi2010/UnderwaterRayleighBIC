The manuscript presents a potentially interesting modal-matching construction of a strict Rayleigh-threshold BIC in a two-groove underwater acoustic metagrating. The final retained calculation is reproducible at (N,K)=(313,39), including the reduced-operator residual and the direct full-operator endpoint check, and the distinction between homogeneous and driven fields is conceptually important. In its present form, however, the evidence and figure bookkeeping are not yet sufficient for a PRL submission. I recommend major revision focused on matching every plotted datum to the final geometry and truncation, adding the missing homogeneous-field and tolerance evidence, and narrowing all device-level claims to what the lossless infinite-period model actually demonstrates.
1. The strict BIC criterion is the strongest part of the paper, but the manuscript should make the non-circular verification explicit. In `arxiv_theory_paper/main.tex`, Eqs. (\ref{eq:strict-zero}) and (\ref{eq:strict-diagnostics}) describe deletion of the threshold and finite-flux columns followed by exact reinsertion of zeros. The audit reproduces the reduced result, sigma_min/sigma_max=1.2603643174e-15 and r_raw=1.3765667611e-13, but the independent full complex operator at the exact final endpoint gives sigma_min/sigma_max=7.7151137924e-16 and a strict-mode raw residual of 1.3765667769e-13 (`advanced_analysis/reproduction_raw/16_full_operator_final_endpoint.txt`). These full-operator numbers should be shown or clearly tabulated, together with the explicit threshold classification/tolerance. The audit found that floating-point ky,-1=1.1921e-7 can be larger than the default classifier tolerance 5.7563e-8 even though Omega=1-kappa; this is a numerical classification issue, not a physical failure, but the manuscript must state how the exact normalized Rayleigh condition and the operator tolerance are imposed.
2. The pole evidence must be tied to the same final root as the strict-null evidence. The current text around Eq. (\ref{eq:complex-q}) and the caption of Fig. \ref{fig:bic-pole} call the curve a cached pole continuation, while the cache is the intermediate (N,K)=(121,15) root with kappa=0.0839406368487959, not the final (313,39) geometry (`advanced_analysis/reproduction_audit.md`, section “Claim-by-claim verification table”). A same-geometry continuation is now available from `advanced_analysis/final_root_pole/final_root_pole_track.csv`, generated by `advanced_analysis/final_root_pole_validation.m`: it remains on one tracked branch, has minimum mode overlap 0.999999500, endpoint full sigma 7.7151137924e-16, Q=2.0261339296e12 at |Delta kappa|=1e-5, and local log-log slope -1.99590867. The main figure and supplement should use this track or label the old curve unambiguously as an intermediate-truncation diagnostic. The Q law must remain explicitly a resolved finite-model trend rather than a continuum asymptotic exponent.
3. The truncation evidence should distinguish reoptimized roots from a fixed geometry evaluated at changing truncation. `advanced_analysis/extended_convergence/extended_root_sequence.csv` shows reoptimized strict roots through (N,K)=(377,47), with machine-level reduced and full residuals at each reoptimized point and a decreasing maximum parameter step, reaching 7.7654e-6 at K=47. In contrast, `fixed_high_root_crosscheck.csv` gives nonzero residuals of order 10^-5 when the highest-K geometry is evaluated at neighboring truncations, while the exact K=47 evaluation is machine-level. This is useful evidence of a converged sequence, but it does not justify presenting one discretization as a continuum proof. Fig. 2 and its caption should show both tests with their meanings stated, and the text should report the truncation sequence and remaining continuum/model limitations.
4. The driven-router convergence table is not reliable in its current form and should not appear as evidence of a converged device response. Table \ref{tab:s-strict-convergence} in `arxiv_theory_paper/suppl.tex` lists eta_anom=0.30737, 0.30662, 0.30620, and 0.30613 for stored frequencies, but direct fixed-final-geometry reruns give 0.0005427505, 0.005038793, 0.1468842, and 0.3060611 at (121,15), (185,23), (281,35), and (313,39), respectively (`advanced_analysis/reproduction_audit.md`, “Direct driven route data at fixed frequency”). At fixed frequency the (401,50) value falls to 0.0571528, exposing strong truncation sensitivity. The table should be removed or regenerated by a documented joint retuning procedure that reports geometry, frequency, condition number, and energy error at every truncation. The final finite-truncation route may be retained as an illustrative calculation with eta_-1=0.3060611, eta_0=0.6939389, but it is not evidence for a converged one-way device. The reciprocity equations in Eq. (\ref{eq:unitarity-reciprocity}) correctly imply signed-order exchange rather than isolation; the manuscript should not use “single-direction,” “unidirectional,” or “isolator” language for this reciprocal system.
5. The current eigenfield evidence does not support the stated three-way field interpretation. The paragraph preceding Fig. \ref{fig:fields} says that the figure labels a normalized homogeneous eigenfield separately, but the figure manifest (`arxiv_theory_paper/figures/figure_manifest.txt`) identifies panel (d) as a cached driven pressure field and does not provide strict-BIC, near-BIC, and ordinary-resonance homogeneous maps. A PRL reader needs identical-grid, consistently normalized |p(x,y)| maps for the strict BIC, a controlled near-BIC pole, and an ordinary resonance, with the open/threshold amplitudes and exterior localization stated in the caption. A real-axis smallest-SVD-vector surrogate must be labeled as such; an exact real-frequency square-integrable eigenfield is not available for an ordinary leaky resonance. The requested diagnostic is now implemented in `advanced_analysis/robustness_fields.m`; field_states.csv, fig_homogeneous_fields.pdf, robustness_fields.mat, fig_dof_comparison.pdf, fig_fabrication_tolerance.pdf, and robustness_fields_report.md exist. They still require independent numerical/visual inspection and consistent integration into the manuscript before they can be cited as complete PRL evidence.
6. The two-cavity degree-of-freedom argument is plausible but bounded. The manuscript’s statement that a second cavity is “required” should remain qualified as “within the searched rectangular family.” The cached single-cavity result is a search result at (N,K)=(221,44), with sigma_min/sigma_max=0.1245882 and raw residual 1.48399; the two-groove value is 1.2604e-15 and 1.3766e-13 at (313,39) (`advanced_analysis/robustness_fields/dof_comparison.csv` and `advanced_analysis/reproduction_audit.md`). The search bounds, free variables, treatment of transverse modes, and optimization objective should be stated in the supplement. Showing the two complex phasor closures is valuable, but it does not establish a universal one-cavity no-go theorem; arbitrary internal structure or a different cavity shape could supply additional phase degrees of freedom.
7. The scaling and environmental section should separate exact similarity from robustness of a fixed fabricated device. `advanced_analysis/scaling_environment_report.md` and its CSVs establish the conditional law f_BIC(c,s)=f_0(c/1500\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})/s when all normalized dimensions and the incidence angle are retained: the retuned sweep gives 186.666667--213.333333 kHz over c=1400--1600 m/s and machine-level residuals. The scale sweep gives 400--100 kHz for scale factors 0.5--2. A fixed geometry and f=200 kHz, however, has full raw residual up to 0.649 and open-amplitude ratio up to 0.776 away from c=1500 m/s. The paper should include the distinction and avoid calling the similarity result environmental tolerance, temperature robustness, or scale-independent experimental performance. Loss, dispersion, finite width, and source/aperture effects remain untested.
8. The fabrication-tolerance analysis is not yet submission evidence. The present CSVs contain 100 samples per mode and level at (N,K)=(185,23), but they mix a balanced one-parameter “independent” ensemble with a five-parameter “joint” ensemble; the independent ensemble is not a random perturbation of all five dimensions. The physically meaningful threshold error is the unconstrained full-operator |A_-1|/||A||, whereas the strict operator’s threshold amplitude is exactly zero by construction; this distinction must be retained in the caption and methods. The tolerance CSVs, vector figure, and robustness_fields_report.md now exist, but the study still needs condition-number accounting, explicit fixed-versus-retuned wording in the manuscript, and independent review of the distribution definitions before they are cited as fabrication robustness or acceptance probabilities.
9. The off-Rayleigh 99.96% result is reproducible at (N,K)=(401,50), with eta_target=0.9996185039, eta_0=0.0003814961, and energy closure 4.44e-16, but the convergence sequence is nonmonotonic at the last step (0.9999151 at (313,39) versus 0.9996185 at (401,50)). It should be presented as a separate ordinary anomalous-reflection state, not as evidence that the Rayleigh BIC enhances efficiency. A frequency/angle linewidth or stability analysis should use a converged truncation and report the condition number; otherwise this result is peripheral and can be moved to the supplement.
10. The manuscript should explicitly delimit the model before making device-level claims. The final paragraph of `main.tex` correctly lists rigid, lossless, nondispersive fluid and infinite periodicity, but the paper still needs a quantitative discussion of finite-array leakage, thermoviscous loss, fluid-structure compliance, source aperture, disorder, and the meaning of a measured “BIC” in a finite tank. The proposed protocol in Figs. \ref{fig:s-protocol} and \ref{fig:s-controls} is appropriately labeled as future work, but it is not a validation result. Author names and affiliations are also still placeholders in both TeX files and must be completed before any submission package is called final.
